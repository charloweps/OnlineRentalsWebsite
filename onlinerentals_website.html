How it works

A straightforward rental management workflow

OnlineRentals is designed around the everyday sequence of managing rental properties.

01

Set up your properties

Organize your properties, plots and available rental units so that your portfolio has a clear structure.

02

Register tenants

Create or maintain tenant records and assign tenants to the appropriate available units.

03

Manage billing

Configure applicable rental charges and recurring bills, then monitor invoices and tenant balances.

04

Track payments

Record payments and keep tenant financial records current as rental activity continues.

05

Monitor deposits

Keep track of rental deposits and relevant deposit activity throughout a tenancy.

06

Manage operations

Handle day-to-day property operations while keeping information connected to the appropriate property and unit.

07

Review finances

Monitor revenue, expenditures, outstanding amounts and other financial information.

08

Make informed decisions

Use the information available in the system to understand occupancy, collections and property performance.

Different responsibilities

Designed for the people involved in rental management

Different users can work with the information relevant to their responsibilities.

Administrators

Oversee system operations, users and the broader property management environment.

Property Staff

Handle day-to-day property, tenant, billing and operational activities assigned to them.

Landlords

Monitor the properties, rental activity and financial information relevant to their portfolio.

Caretakers

Support property-level operations and activities assigned to their responsibilities.

Agents

Work with the properties and rental responsibilities assigned to them.

Tenants

Access the information and functions available to them in relation to their tenancy.

Access & security

The right information for the right user.

Property management involves sensitive financial and tenant information. OnlineRentals is designed so that access can be aligned with the responsibilities of individual users.

Controlled access

Users can work within the areas of the platform relevant to their responsibilities.

Property-level boundaries

Access can be limited to the properties and units relevant to a user's role.

Organized information

Property, tenant and financial records are kept within a centralized management system.

Account-based access

Each user works through their own authenticated account.
