Help and information

Frequently asked questions.

An introduction to OnlineRentals and the kinds of work it is designed to support.

OnlineRentals is a property management application designed to help organize rental properties, tenant records, billing, payment records, expenses and financial reporting.
It is intended to support property owners and management teams, with access for other users where the organization has configured it. The functions available to an individual depend on their account and assigned permissions.
The application supports organizing property and rental-unit records. The properties visible to your account depend on the access assigned to you.
Available billing features support rental charges, recurring monthly charges and invoice records. Applicable amounts and balances depend on the information recorded for the relevant tenancy.
The application supports invoice balances and payment records, including situations where an account has an outstanding amount or a credit balance. The displayed position depends on the records maintained in the system.
Deposit and refund records, as well as expenditure and financial reporting, are among the management tasks supported by the application.
Access is based on account permissions and assigned responsibilities. If information you need is missing, contact your organization's administrator to confirm that your account has the appropriate access.
If you already have an account, use the Sign In link. For a new account, access issues or technical support, contact your system administrator or the support contact provided by your organization.
